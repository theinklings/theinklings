Our Purpose

To make cool stuff together.

The Inklings is designed to be an organized, compact, and relaxed community of creators who are dedicated to helping others imagine, build, and grow.

Whether you are working on a massive project, launching a creative hobby or just want to share advice, we're here to hit the grind and create meaningful works, as well as helping you host your own creative events.

No fluff, no pressure. Just pure, unadulterated creative pursuit alongside people who care about their craft.

Community Pillars

Three core activities that define what we do every single day as a community.

Imagine

Concept feedback, creative jams, storytelling review, and visual brainstorming. We push the boundaries of what is possible, bringing raw sketches into logical frameworks.

Brainstorm Info

Build

This is where we hit the grind. Co-working sprints, code hacking sessions, collaborative design, and project shipping. We build things that work, and help debug along the way.

Sprint With Us

Grow

Reviewing shipped items, seeking mentorship, hosting workshops, and sharing advice. We support each other's career progression and professional or artistic growth.
