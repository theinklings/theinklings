Our Story

How we came to be.

February 3, 2024

The Art Society for Illustration, Design, and Expression

Originally founded under the name the Art Society for Illustration, Design, and Expression—or "Artside" for short—this group was established as a dedicated, welcoming hub where artists of all kinds could gather to create and express themselves freely. This initiative was a passionate attempt to revive an earlier organization known as the Society for Thai Artists and Students (STAS).

Early 2025

A Period of Silence

Despite the founders' initial intentions, the first iteration of Artside struggled to maintain consistent momentum, eventually falling entirely quiet in early 2025 before dissolving completely. A period of dormancy followed.

May 27, 2026

The Inklings — Reborn

The group successfully found its footing again, officially rebranding as The Inklings and introducing a profound shift in its core philosophical focus. Instead of operating strictly as a club for traditional artists, it transformed into a close-knit community centered around imagination and creativity as tools to help people do good in the world. The Inklings has since established multiple regional sectors across different countries, hosting localized community events and shared creative activities.
